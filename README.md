# Coffee-Sales-Performance-Dashboard-2025
Coffee-Sales-Performance-Analysis  
This project presents an analysis of coffee sales performance across products, months, payment methods, weekdays, and time periods. The objective is to identify purchasing patterns, top-performing products, peak sales periods, and customer payment preferences.

🔍 Overview

The Coffee Sales Performance Dashboard provides an interactive view of sales performance throughout the year.

The dashboard analyzes:

* Coffee product performance
* Monthly sales
* Sales by weekday
* Sales by time of day
* Payment methods
* Product contribution to overall sales

The analysis helps identify the products and periods contributing most to sales and provides insights that can support inventory planning and promotional strategies.

🎯 Objectives

* Identify the best-selling coffee products.
* Analyze monthly sales trends.
* Determine the strongest sales days.
* Identify peak sales periods throughout the day.
* Understand customer payment preferences.
* Provide actionable recommendations for improving sales.

❓ Business Questions

* Which coffee product generates the highest sales?
* Which month records the highest sales?
* What payment method is most preferred?
* Which weekday generates the most sales?
* What time of day records the highest sales?
* Which products contribute most to overall sales?

📚 Dataset Description

Dataset: Coffee sales transaction data
Data Type: Sales transaction data

Key Fields:

* Coffee Product
* Sales
* Month
* Weekday
* Time of Day
* Payment Method
* Transaction details

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

* Microsoft Excel

* Data Cleaning

🧹 Data Cleaning and Preparation

* Removed duplicate records.
* Standardized product names.
* Checked numerical sales values.
* Organized transaction dates into months and weekdays.
* Categorized transaction times.
* Standardized payment method values.
* Prepared the dataset for PivotTable analysis.

📈 Key Trends Discovered

* March recorded the highest monthly sales.
* Latte was the highest-selling coffee, contributing 23.94%.
* Americano with Milk followed with 22.05%.
* Night recorded the highest sales by time of day.
* Tuesday recorded the highest weekday sales.
* Card was the most-used payment method.

📊 Dashboard Development

The dashboard presents:

* Monthly sales analysis
* Coffee product performance
* Payment method distribution
* Sales by weekday
* Sales by time of day
* Product contribution

💡 Recommendations

* Maintain sufficient inventory for top-selling products such as Latte.
* Introduce promotions during peak periods.
* Replicate successful March sales strategies.
* Target Tuesday customers with special offers.
* Continue supporting convenient card-payment options.

Conclusion

The analysis shows that coffee sales are influenced by product popularity, timing, and customer purchasing behavior. Latte was the strongest product, while March, Tuesday, and Night represented important sales opportunities.
